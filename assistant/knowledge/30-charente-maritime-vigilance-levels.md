# Charente-Maritime (La Rochelle): the six fire vigilance levels

In Charente-Maritime, the use of fire outdoors is regulated by prefectoral order n° 26EB142.
The order came into force on 1 May 2026.
The previous order, n° 24EB441, was repealed from 1 May 2026.
The order defines six vigilance levels, from lowest to highest.
The levels are: FAIBLE (blue), LÉGER (green), MODÉRÉ (yellow), SÉVÈRE (orange), TRÈS SÉVÈRE (red), EXCEPTIONNEL (black).
Each level brings stronger restrictions.
The levels use Météo-France forecasts and an analysis by the CODIS, the département's fire and rescue operations centre.
The level for a day is shown from 6 pm the day before.
The département is divided into 4 sectors, numbered 1701 to 1704.
La Rochelle is in sector 1 (1701).
Before lighting any fire, a person must identify their sector and its vigilance level.
These levels are different from Météo-France's "Météo des forêts".
The order lists 72 communes in forest areas classified at risk of fire.
