# Knowledge of the chatbot

One line per document of this folder: its file, what it is about, and who checked it.
The chatbot reads this list at the start of each conversation, then opens the documents it needs.

Documents 30 to 32 are copied from the Youth AI Lab workshop kit (folder summaries/). Each one
summarises an official text: the line `resource:` of its header gives the address of that text,
which is not copied here.

* [30-charente-maritime-vigilance-levels.md](30-charente-maritime-vigilance-levels.md) - The six fire vigilance levels of Charente-Maritime (La Rochelle). Checked by a person.
* [31-charente-maritime-fire-use-rules.md](31-charente-maritime-fire-use-rules.md) - What fire use is forbidden in Charente-Maritime, including barbecues. Checked by a person.
* [32-charente-maritime-farm-work-amendment.md](32-charente-maritime-farm-work-amendment.md) - The June 2026 amending order on farm work in Charente-Maritime. Checked by a person.
